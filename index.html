The High Crusade

Ansby, Lincolnshire, Anno Domini 1345

Muster. A strange ship has landed in the east meadow. March your men to it.

How to command

With a mouse

  • Click a soldier to select him. Hold Shift to add more.
  • Drag a box to select a group.
  • Double click to select every soldier of that kind in view.
  • Click or right click the ground to march there.
  • Click or right click an enemy to attack it.
  • Move the view with the arrow keys, the screen edges, or the small map.
  • Scroll to zoom.

With touch

  • Tap a soldier to select him.
  • Press and hold, then drag, to box a group.
  • Double tap to select every soldier of that kind in view.
  • Tap the ground to march there. Tap an enemy to attack it.
  • Drag with one finger to move the view. Pinch to zoom.
